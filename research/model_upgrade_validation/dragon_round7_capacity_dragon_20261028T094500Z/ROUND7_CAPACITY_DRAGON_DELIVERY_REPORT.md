### 问题 3 整改细节：修正容量排名与个股尾盘跳水字段语义

1. **修正 `is_capacity_volume_leader`**：

         (df['is_cohort_volume_leader'] == 1.0) | 
         (df['amount_cohort_rank'] == 1.0) | 
         (df['signal_amount'] >= 8e8)
