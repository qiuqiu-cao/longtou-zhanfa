        1.3 Major changes in main financial items and indexes, and description of the cause
      √Applicable □Inapplicable
         (1). Other current assets decreased by 43.19% compared with the beginning of the year, mainly due to the reduction
        in the input tax amount of value-added tax to be deducted.
         (2). The construction in progress increased by 78.86% compared to the beginning of the year, mainly due to the
       investment for the digital upgrade and transformation of the production line of the subsidiary company Sonyo
       Compressor has increased.
        (3).Other non-current assets increased by 37.25% compared to the beginning of the year, mainly due to the addition
        of properties used for debt settlement in the current period.
         (4). Employee compensation payable has decreased by 48.68% compared to the beginning of the year, mainly due
        to the fact that the employee compensation (year-end bonuses, etc.) accrued at the end of the previous period has
       been paid in the current period.
         (5). Financial expenses increased by 80.35% year-on-year, mainly due to the impact of exchange rate losses.
        (6).The net cash flow from operating activities has increased significantly year-on-year, mainly due to the
        strengthened collection of receivables and the rational matching of cash payments.
         (7). The net cash flow generated from investment activities has increased significantly year-on-year, mainly due to
        the capital investment in fixed assets of the company's subsidiary Sonyo Compressor, as well as the impact of time
        deposits during the same period
